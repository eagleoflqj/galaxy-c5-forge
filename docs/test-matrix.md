1. Powered-on charging: preferably start below 90% after ordinary use, record the percentage, connect a known-working wall charger and confirm the charging indicator. Leave the screen off for 10–15 minutes, then check that the percentage increased and charging stayed connected. Unplug and confirm the indicator clears without a reboot. A full battery cannot establish charging progression.
2. Power-off and offline charging: unplug all cables, select Power off in Android and wait at least 60 seconds. Confirm the phone stays off. Connect the wall charger without pressing a button; verify the offline battery/charging screen appears and the phone does not loop or enter Android. Leave it for 5–10 minutes, then unplug and let the screen go dark for at least 30 seconds.
3. Cable-free cold boot: from that powered-off state, hold only Power until startup begins, then release it. Confirm Android reaches the lock screen/desktop, the existing data/settings remain present, fingerprint unlock works and Wi-Fi reconnects. If startup remains stuck for several minutes or enters recovery, report the screen and elapsed time. Reconnect USB after the manual tests for read-only system verification.
